About

A career built at the intersection of operations and patient care

Above all, patient care comes first. As a Licensed Pharmacist with nearly two decades of frontline and operational healthcare experience — spanning hospital pharmacy, community pharmacy, aged care coordination, mobile imaging services, and remote healthcare administration across the Philippines, Singapore, and Australia — I've built that priority into every part of my work.

My work brings together more than just breadth of experience — it's the ability to hold both the operational machinery and the human being at the center of every healthcare interaction. I've reviewed medication orders end-to-end, coordinated with multicultural clinical teams, trained and mentored new staff on process and compliance standards, and helped redesign workflows that reduced friction and improved care delivery — all without losing sight of the patient on the other end of every decision.

My approach is simple: bring clinical judgment and operational rigor together, so healthcare organizations can work smarter, communicate better, and care more humanely.
